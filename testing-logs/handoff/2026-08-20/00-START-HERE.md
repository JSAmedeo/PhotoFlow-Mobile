Everything needed to write a stakeholder update on this test session.

## How to use

1. Upload **every file in this folder** to a new chat.
2. Say: *"Write a stakeholder update on this test session. Start by asking me what format I need."*
3. The brief tells the assistant the audience, the register, and what not to overstate.
